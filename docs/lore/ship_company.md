# The Carrier's ship's company (the crew who walk the ship)

Canon as of 2026-11-10 (lore developer; owner approved "make changes however you feel is
best"). The place is [LORE.md > The Carrier](../LORE.md#the-carrier); the desk crew and the
Plot's watch officer are in [desk_crew.md](desk_crew.md).

Six people walk the ship while the teams wait. They are the ship's own and the Army staff who
run the lane, never a window team and never Pike grey. Ship's company wear the ship's dark
blue working dress; on the flight deck they wear a coloured jersey and a cloth helmet over it
in the colour of their job, the way a real carrier deck does. Army staff wear Army working
dress. They do not talk to soldiers; a line shows as a small bubble when a player walks near.
No names, no rank on screen.

| Role | Dress | Walks | Overheard (bubble) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plane director | ship; yellow jersey and cloth helmet, ear defenders | the flight deck, spot to spot along the island side | "Stay behind the line. Spot three's turning." / "Mind the tail rotor. You won't see it." |
| Deck hand | ship; blue jersey and cloth helmet, chocks and a tie-down chain over one shoulder | the flight deck, between the spots and the deck edge | "Chocks and chains. Every spot, every time." / "They hose the birds before I touch them." |
| Hangar mechanic | ship; green jersey, coveralls rolled to the waist, a rag in the back pocket | the hangar deck, the ramp foot and along the bay | "Mind your feet. Hose water's in everything down here." / "New tail rotor on that one. Don't ask." |
| Plot runner | ship; dark blue, no jersey, message forms on a clipboard | the hangar deck, from the Plot door to the ramp and back | "Make a hole. Message for the island." / "Plot wants a count. Nobody likes the count." |
| Screening medic | Army; working dress under a white smock, blue gloves, mask round the neck | the screening lane and the deck edge by it | "Hands out, sleeves up. Same as every time." / "Nobody's failed my lane. Let's keep it that way." |
| Lane sentry | Army; working dress, helmet, rifle slung, a white MP brassard | the catwalk along the deck edge above the screening lane, slowly | "Walk the lane slow. I'm paid to watch it." / "Delta had a sentry too. Keep moving." |

Why the lane sentry matters: the lane screens, and if a soldier fails it the sentry is the
answer. He wears the same brassard the Warden wore at Delta. Nobody on the ship says so.
