## What it does

The puff of particles where an Infected dies changes from green to dark blood red. The
player who got the kill also hears a quiet tick together with the red kill marker.

## Findings

No bugs found.

- The change is client-only (it runs on each player's own machine), so there is nothing a
  cheater could gain from it and nothing for the server to check.
- The tick is rate-limited: kills less than 0.07 s apart share one tick
  (`src/client/HitMarkerUI.luau:134-143`, `Rules/Feel.canKillConfirm` with a test).
- Colours, particle count, volume and pitch are in `Config` (`HitMarker.KillBurst`,
  `Audio.KillConfirm`), as the project rules ask.
- The particle burst is the same size as before (14 particles, removed after 1 s), so no new
  performance cost.
- Small note, not a bug: the tick reuses the menu click sound (`ui_click`) played 1.4x
  faster. Whether it sounds different enough from the menu click can only be judged by ear.

## Check in Studio

1. Kill a Walker on dark ground: is the dark red burst still visible?
2. Listen to the tick: soft enough, and clearly not the menu click?
3. Hold the trigger on the M249 into a crowd, then drop an Anvil on a group: the ticks
   should not turn into a buzz.
4. Let a bot get a kill next to you: you should see the burst but hear no tick.
