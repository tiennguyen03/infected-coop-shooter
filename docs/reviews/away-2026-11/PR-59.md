**Verdict: Fix first** (two small fixes: the Doorman's damage number, and text too small on

## What it does

The loadout screen (at the lobby's Loadmaster and in the match's Arms Room) gets bigger. Each
row now shows the item's shape, and the item you pick turns in 3D beside a card of its stats with
bars comparing it to the rest. The stats come from a new tested rule, `Rules/KitCard`.

## Findings

1. **The Doorman sentry looks like the weakest call-in** (sure). `KitCard.callIn` works out
   a call-in's total damage as damage × shells/bursts/mines, or damage per second × duration
   (`src/shared/Rules/KitCard.luau:87-115`). The Doorman has `Damage = 20`, `FireInterval =
   0.15` and `Duration = 45` but none of those counts, so its card says "Damage 20" with 1 of
   10 bars, next to totals of 480 to 1200 for the others. Players will skip it. Smallest fix:
   when a call-in has `FireInterval`, treat it as per second (`Damage / FireInterval`), and add
   a test for it.
2. **Text too small on phones** (sure about the arithmetic, needs a look on a phone). On a
   phone screen (844 × 390) the compact layout is shrunk to about 0.6 of its size, so the
   size-13 hint line (`src/client/LoadoutMenu.luau:416`) comes out at about 8 pixels, and
   labels, tabs and stats at 8.5-10 pixels. `docs/STYLES.md` sets 11-12 as the minimum. Rows
   end up about 42-45 pixels tall, at or under the 44-pixel minimum for a finger. Fix: bigger
   sizes on the compact layout, or never shrink below a set floor.
3. **"[B] BACK" shown to controller players in the match, but B does nothing there** (sure).
   The hint adds "[B] BACK" whenever a controller is connected (`LoadoutMenu.luau:413-415`), but
   the match's `LoadoutUI` only listens for the keyboard B key (`src/client/LoadoutUI.luau:255`).
   Fix: show it only where a back action exists, or bind controller B in `LoadoutUI`.
4. Possible: a future grenade missing a field (say `FuseTime`) would read "0 s" with a full bar
   (`KitCard.luau:243-266`). Only the Frag exists today and it has every field.
5. Possible: on a controller, switching tabs with L1/R1 hides the old rows but does not move
   the selection (`LoadoutMenu.luau:930-942`), so A might pick an item you cannot see.
6. Possible, not measured: 18 small 3D views (one per row) plus one turning view. Each costs
   little per frame, but nobody measured it on a phone (the budget is 1 ms per frame). The row
   shapes are on by default on phones too. Fix: turn them off on touch devices, or measure.
7. Small: camera numbers (`FOV`, `ELEVATION`, `MAX_FIT`, screen margins) are constants in
   `LoadoutMenu.luau:46-48, 390` rather than in `Config`, as the project rules ask.
8. Docs: STYLES and HISTORY say the screen is 1560 × 880 at 1080p, but the fit can grow it to
   about 1.1×, about 1700 × 970. The row-shape size on the compact layout is also different from
   the doc.
9. Older, not from this PR: in the lobby, with the Gunsmith open, controller B closes the
   Loadmaster underneath and leaves the Gunsmith's cover and turning loop behind
   (`GunsmithUI.luau:186, 260-283`).

Fine: nothing leaks. Every per-frame loop, listener and key binding is removed when the screen
closes, and the old 3D model is deleted before the next one. The two server changes only start
the new `Showcase` module, which puts look-only models in ReplicatedStorage. It adds no
remote and reads nothing from players. It works in both places (the lobby project includes
all of `src/shared`). Per-item values (`Show`, `Model`) sit on each item's own row with a
fallback.
