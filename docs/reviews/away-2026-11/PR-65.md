## What it does

It adds the first vehicle: a quad bike (ATV) you call in, which a helicopter lowers on a
sling. One soldier drives and another rides on the back and shoots; bots can ride on the back
too. In the lobby there is a new "motor pool" display where it is sold.

## Findings

1. **One enemy hit damages the ATV once per rider** (sure). Each rider hit calls the vehicle
   damage again (`src/server/EnemyProjectileService.luau:111-120`), and Hocker spit and the
   Warden's area attacks loop over every soldier in range (for example the splash at line
   169-171). So one hit that covers both seats costs the ATV double. Trigger: two soldiers on
   one ATV, a Warden pound: about 300 of its 400 health in one hit. Smallest fix: in
   `onRiderHit` (`src/server/VehicleService.luau:545`), ignore a second hit on the same ATV in
   the same frame.
2. **The landing spot is checked only when you throw, and ignores soldiers and other ATVs**
   (sure). The "is there room" box (`VehicleService.luau:676-692`) leaves out the vehicles
   folder and every soldier, also skips invisible walls (line 688), and `deliver` (line 702)
   spawns the ATV 7.5 seconds later without checking again. Trigger: throw it onto a
   teammate's parked ATV, or a soldier walks onto the mark: the new ATV appears inside them and
   may fling them. Fix: include the vehicles and soldiers in the check, and check again in
   `deliver` (refund or lift the spot if blocked).
3. **A cheating driver can move the ATV anywhere and ram from there** (sure about the code;
   only a cheater can trigger it). The driver's game controls the ATV's physics
   (`VehicleService.luau:200-209`), which is normal for Roblox vehicles. The server checks
   the ATV's speed (lines 889-900) but never where it is. A modified client could jump the ATV
   next to Infected and ram them for about 125 damage each every 0.6 s, carrying a teammate.
   Fix: remember the position each slow tick; if it moved faster than top speed allows, take
   control back and put it back.
4. **Two ATVs can fight over the same bot** (likely, co-op with bots). `boardBot`
   (`VehicleService.luau:286-326`) does not skip a bot that another ATV is already calling,
   so the bot's target flips between them, and one ATV can cancel the other's call. Comments
   and docs say "in solo", but the code does not check that. Fix: skip bots already being
   called, or limit it to solo.
5. Possible: getting off in a narrow alley can put you in a wall. If both sides are blocked,
   the rider is still put on the blocked left side (`VehicleService.luau:236-244`). Fix: put
   them on top of the ATV instead.
6. Possible: the ATV model is not marked to stream in as one piece (`ModelStreamingMode
   Atomic`, which `docs/STYLES.md` asks for moving rigs). A half-loaded ATV could keep a
   wrong wheel list on a player's screen (`VehicleController.luau:162-183`).
7. Small: with a second charge or a free call, a player can get past the squad's ATV limit
   while their first one is still in the air (`VehicleService.luau:661-696`). Charges are 1
   today, so this is hard to reach.
8. Possible: bot gunners' shots are not set to pass through ATVs (BotService's ray filter,
   about line 405), so a bot can shoot the ATV it is near instead of the Infected.
9. Possible: the driver's controls do not stop during a pause, so the ATV keeps driving.
10. Rules: some tuning numbers sit at the top of `VehicleService.luau` (lines 48-56) instead
    of in Config; the attribute names `"Mount"` and `"Front"` are typed out in
    `VehicleBody.luau:260-261` and `VehicleController.luau:170-175` instead of in
    `Attributes.luau`; a damaged ATV smokes from always-on emitters, even when parked, where
    STYLES asks for bursts.
11. Docs: `docs/PROGRESSION.md:371` says a bot boards "within 25 studs", but Config says 40;
    PROGRESSION says "not played" while the handoff log says Nova played it; the
    VehicleService header says seats are picked by `Rules/Vehicle.seatFor`, which the server
    never calls.

Fine: a seat only works through its own prompt, and walking into one does nothing. The server
refuses the driver's shots, checks the gunner's angle, and blocks grenades, call-ins, flare and
revive from a seat. Getting off only accepts the exact "Exit" message for the sender's own seat.
Ram and blast damage go through `DamageService.dealDamage`. Control goes back to the server
when the driver leaves or dies. Rides are cleaned up when a run starts and when a player leaves.
The Studio-only "ATV in your loadout" is locked to Studio (`LoadoutService.luau:87`). The engine
sound is an uploaded asset id, not a file in the repository. The new rule `Rules/Vehicle` has
its tests.

## Check in Studio

1. Two players on one ATV, let a Hocker spit or the Warden hit both: does the ATV lose health
   once or twice (finding 1)?
2. Throw the ATV onto a parked ATV, and onto a teammate standing on the mark (finding 2).
3. Drive into a narrow alley and get off; let an ATV burn out with both seats full (riders
   thrown off, the wreck stays put, you can call another).
4. 2 players + 2 bots, both players on ATVs near one bot: does it get stuck between them
   (finding 4)?
5. MicroProfiler with two ATVs moving (the log says this was not measured).
