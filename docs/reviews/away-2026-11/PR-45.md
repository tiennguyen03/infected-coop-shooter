## What it does

When you vault, climb or roll, Infected melee cannot hit you for a short moment. This PR
moves the small calculation of that moment out of `CoverMoveService` into a shared rule file
with four tests. The numbers and the behaviour are the same.

## Findings

No bugs found.

- The new function (`src/shared/Rules/CoverWindow.luau:12-23`) does exactly what the old
  lines did: refuse if the last window plus the cooldown has not passed, otherwise end the
  window at now + move time + grace.
- `src/server/CoverMoveService.luau:54-69` passes the same `Config.Movement` values, and the
  checks before it (message type, player alive) are untouched, so a player still cannot
  chain moves to stay immune.

## Check in Studio

1. Vault a barrier with a Walker swinging at you: the hit should still miss during the vault.
2. Roll twice in a row quickly next to a Walker: the second roll should not protect you.
