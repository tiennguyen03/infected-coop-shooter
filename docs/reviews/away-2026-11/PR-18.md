# PR #18: Call-in arrivals: every call-in comes in its own way

- Author: tiennguyen03 (branch `claude/callin-arrivals`)
- Head commit reviewed: `60c9247ed5100bd5f8e8c29d9a15955099cc186d`
- Reviewed: 2026-11-11 (by reading the code; the tests were not run here)

**Verdict: Looks safe to merge** (only small visual edge cases; it is a big visual change,
so play it first).

## What it does

Each call-in now has its own arrival that every player sees: marks on the ground, incoming
rounds, parachutes, rolling wire drums, smoke and dust. Things that are placed (sentry,
mines, wire, supply drops) now appear a moment later (1 to 2.2 s), when the arrival lands.

## Findings

No crash, cheat or leak found. All findings are low.

1. **Possible: on the Shrike gun run, the dust and tracers drawn on your screen slowly run
   ahead of the real hits.** The server waits between bursts one after another
   (`src/server/AirStrikeService.luau:393-416`), and each wait
   (`src/server/PauseService.luau:31-39`) finishes up to one frame late; the lateness adds
   up over 11 waits. The client uses exact times (`src/client/CallInArrivalFX.luau:561`).
   Expect about 0.1 s by the last burst normally and 0.2-0.35 s when the server is busy.
   Trigger: throw a Shrike with 40+ Infected alive and watch the last few bursts.
   How sure: the cause is verified by reading; whether it is visible needs a playtest.
   Smallest fix: make `PauseService.wait` return how much it overshot, and subtract that
   from the next wait in `gunRun`.

2. **With many call-ins at once, the oldest arrival is removed even if its shells are still
   in the air.** `src/client/CallInArrivalFX.luau:993-995` keeps at most 8
   (`Config.luau:845`, `MaxActive = 8`) and always removes the oldest. An arrival keeps its
   place for a long time (smoke about 6 s, the Anvil's scorch mark 15 s).
   Trigger: four players throw 9 or more call-ins within about 6 s: the first one's ground
   marks or falling case vanish, while its real effect still lands. Visual only.
   How sure: verified by reading. Smallest fix: raise `MaxActive` to 12 (each arrival is at
   most 22 parts), or remove the arrival with the least left to show instead of the oldest.

3. **If the thrower leaves the game during the 1-2 s wait, a supply drop never appears.**
   `src/server/CallInService.luau:84` (`if thisGeneration == generation and player.Parent`).
   The squad has already been shown the bundle coming down (`:222`), and the supply drop
   does not need the thrower at all (`:218`).
   Trigger: a player throws an Aid Bag and disconnects within 1.8 s.
   How sure: verified by reading. Smallest fix: skip the `player.Parent` check for the
   Speedball and Aid Bag; keep it for sentry, mines and wire (they belong to the thrower).

4. Small: during a pause (run end or the dev panel) the marker smoke keeps its real-time
   timer and can stop early (`CallInArrivalFX.luau:166`, `:248`). Cosmetic.

5. Small, project rule: a few sound names and numbers are written inside
   `CallInArrivalFX.luau` (lines 293, 448, 435, 673, 694, 802) instead of on the call-in's
   `Config` row. Not a bug; it matters when the real mortar and cannon sounds arrive.

6. Small, docs: the Hammer's `Config` comment says "a cross", the code draws a diamond.
   `docs/PROGRESSION.md` says the blast sound and flash come "from the row"; for the Shrike
   they are still fixed in `AirStrikeService.luau:409-411`.

Checked and fine:
- The new message `CallInArrival` only goes from server to players. The check on who may
  throw a call-in (alive, has a charge, not paused) is unchanged.
- A run restart cancels anything still waiting to appear (server) and clears every effect
  (client).
- The server's delay and the client's picture use the same numbers from the same `Config`
  rows and the same shared rule (`Rules/Arrival`, with tests).
- Performance: no lights, particles only in short bursts, at most 22 parts per arrival, one
  per-frame loop over at most 8 arrivals; nothing is left behind after an arrival ends.
