# PR #28: The Anvil is marked through binoculars, anywhere in the line of sight

- Author: tiennguyen03 (branch `claude/anvil-binoculars`)
- Head commit reviewed: `e3141420b914240af754be7323013f1243a9de07`
- Reviewed: 2026-11-11 (by reading the code; the tests were not run here). Only the part
  added on top of #18 was reviewed; #18's findings are in `PR-18.md`. This branch does not
  contain #25.

**Verdict: Fix first** (the cheat protection is good; there is one stuck-state bug, and it
needs care when merged with #25).

## What it does

The Anvil's key now raises binoculars (zoomed view from the soldier's head); a left click
marks the ground you are looking at, up to 400 studs away, and the strike lands there. The
server checks the mark again from the soldier's own head before accepting it.

## Findings

1. **Getting knocked down with the binoculars up leaves you zoomed in, and can leave your
   soldier stuck in the binocular pose for everyone else.**
   A knockdown (the Warden's pound or stomp) does not change the soldier's state from
   "Alive", and neither side lowers the binoculars for it: the server only lowers on a state
   change (`src/server/CallInService.luau:467-471`) and the client only listens for respawn,
   downed and pause (`src/client/BinocularController.luau:397-407`). If the player then
   clicks, the client lowers without telling the server (`:286-287`, `lowerNow(false)`),
   and the server ignores the click because a knocked player cannot act (`:350-353`), before
   it reaches its own `lower(player)` at `:383`. The server-side "binoculars up" flag stays
   on, so other players keep seeing that soldier with arms raised and the gun hidden until
   they next raise the binoculars, go down, or the run restarts.
   Trigger: raise the Anvil binoculars, get knocked down by the Warden, left-click.
   How sure: verified by reading. Smallest fix: on the server, lower the binoculars at the
   very top of the call-in handler (before the "can act" check); on the client, cancel when
   the `KnockedUntil` attribute is set.

2. **Merging with #25 needs a deliberate choice, or marked strikes break.** A trial merge
   conflicts in `src/server/CallInService.luau`, `src/client/AbilityController.luau`,
   `docs/HISTORY.md` and `handoff/README.md`. #25 sends every "thrown" call-in through a
   0.5 s throw wind-up; the Anvil is still a "thrown" kind here
   (`src/shared/CallInDefinitions.luau:54`). If #25's code is kept as is, the Anvil strike is
   delayed by a throw nobody sees, and a mark made while a grenade is winding up is silently
   dropped. How sure: verified by reading both branches.
   Fix at merge time: skip the wind-up when the call-in is binocular-marked
   (`if definition.Kind == "throw" and not marked then ThrowWindUp.begin(...) else use() end`),
   and keep the binocular check before it.

3. **Possible: far marks may fail on weaker devices.** The Anvil's range is 400 studs
   (`src/shared/Config.luau:986`), but the map only loads 384 studs around each player and
   as little as 128 on a low-memory device (`default.project.json:7-9`). Ground that is not
   loaded reads "NO GROUND"; a wall that is not loaded could let the player make a mark the
   server then refuses. How sure: numbers verified; the effect needs a low-end test.
   Fix: lower the range, or accept it and write it down.

4. **A refused mark tells the player nothing.** The server only writes to its log
   (`CallInService.luau:388-396`); the binoculars have already come down. No charge is lost.
   Two honest ways to be refused: lowering and re-raising quickly and then clicking within
   0.25 s; and looking just over the top of a wall or ridge (the client measures from the
   camera, the server from the head). How sure: possible.
   Smallest fix: send the player a "refused" message so the HUD can say so.

5. Small hardening: add an explicit check that the mark is a normal number before using it
   (`if point ~= point or point.Magnitude > 1e6 then return end`). Today a broken value is
   only rejected indirectly.

6. Small: the comments and docs mention touch and gamepad controls, but only mouse and
   keyboard are handled (`BinocularController.luau:381-391`). `CallInService.spotFor`
   (`:303-341`, about 40 lines) is not called by anything yet.

Checked and fine (the important part for a remote-aimed strike):
- A player's machine cannot land the strike wherever it likes. The server measures again
  from that player's own head: range limit, the point must be on real ground, and nothing
  may block the line of sight. Marks through walls or across the map are refused.
- The server checks the sender is alive, not paused, has the Anvil in that slot, has a
  charge, and has had the binoculars up long enough. The charge is spent exactly once; a
  refused mark spends nothing; spamming is limited to about 4 tries a second.
- Respawn, going down, pause and changing the slot all lower the binoculars and restore the
  camera and gun. The marking click does not fire the gun.
- Per-frame cost is 3-4 ray checks only while your own binoculars are up.
- Names are in `Remotes` and `Attributes`; the rule is pure with a test file; the Anvil's
  range is on its own `Config` row.

## Check in Studio

1. Raise the Anvil in third person and first person (T), and press T while it is up: camera,
   body, gun and crosshair should all come back, and the gun should work again.
2. Get knocked down by the Warden with the binoculars up, then click (finding 1). Also go
   down, die, respawn and pause while they are up: no stuck black mask or zoom.
3. Mark at 300-400 studs, over a wall top, through a doorway, at a wall, at the sky. Watch
   the Output for "mark refused" (findings 3 and 4).
4. Two players: does the other player see the binoculars go up and come down?
5. How does the mouse feel at that zoom? Lower, re-raise quickly and click.
