# PR #21: Stamina and bar; slides keep momentum

- Author: tiennguyen03 (branch `claude/stamina`)
- Head commit reviewed: `14f5a6c5542426a0599040abe971658064b1bcaf`
- Reviewed: 2026-11-11 (by reading the code; the tests were not run here)

**Verdict: Needs a decision** (the code is sound, with one fix to make; but it changes what
the movement keys do when you are out of breath, and `docs/OWNER.md` says the owner decides
movement keys).

## What it does

Sprinting now uses up a stamina bar (about 8 s of sprint; it refills after 1 s of rest; a
roll costs 20, a slide 10; at zero you are "winded" until it is back to 25). Slides now keep
their speed and can run up to 6 s down a slope instead of always lasting 0.95 s.

## Findings

1. **Getting downed or knocked over during a slide on a ramp locks your body for up to 6 s.**
   `src/client/MovementController.luau:219-223`: a slide is only cancelled when
   `humanoid.Health <= 0`, but a downed soldier keeps 1 health (`Config.Downed.DownedHealth =
   1`). On `main` this cost at most 0.95 s. Now the slide lasts until its own speed number
   runs out or 6 s pass (`Config.Movement.Slide.MaxDuration`), and on a slope the speed keeps
   being topped up. During that time the game keeps pushing the body down the ramp
   (`:256`, `humanoid:Move(current.direction)`) at crawl speed and the player cannot steer.
   Trigger: slide down a ramp of about 16 degrees or more and get downed mid-slide.
   How sure: verified by reading (the 6 s case needs a slope; on flat ground it is under 1 s,
   as before). Smallest fix at line 220:
   ```lua
   local model = current.humanoid.Parent
   if not current.root.Parent or current.humanoid.Health <= 0
       or (model and model:GetAttribute(Attributes.SquadState) == "Downed")
       or (model and model:GetAttribute(Attributes.KnockedUntil) ~= nil) then
       endDash(current)
       return
   end
   ```

2. **Decision for the owner: when the bar is empty, the slide/roll keys make you crouch or
   go prone instead.** `src/client/SprintController.luau:92-94` makes `isSprinting()` false
   while winded, and `src/client/StanceController.luau:123-126` only blocks crouch/prone on C
   and Left Ctrl while `isSprinting()` is true. So: sprint until empty with Shift held, press
   C to slide away from the horde, and the soldier lies down. The PR's note says this is on
   purpose. No key binding is changed, but what the keys do is.
   Also (verified by reading): a roll that empties the bar makes you winded at once
   (`MovementController.luau:289-292`), so tapping Left Ctrl or C again during that roll
   toggles crouch or prone mid-roll.
   Smallest fix if not wanted: in `StanceController.luau:124`, ignore C / Left Ctrl while
   Shift is held or while a roll or slide is in progress, whatever the stamina.

3. **Stamina is not enforced by the server.** It lives only on the player's own machine
   (`SprintController.luau:31`); no server file is touched. A cheater can ignore it. This is
   no worse than `main`, where the player's machine already controls its own run speed, and
   the PR's docs say so. Just be aware it is a fairness rule for honest players only.

4. Small, looks only: a slide that starts on flat ground and reaches a ramp later keeps
   sliding in the normal run animation (`MovementController.luau:241-249`).

Checked and fine:
- Drain, refill and slide physics use the frame time correctly, so they behave the same at
  30 and 144 frames per second.
- Respawn refills the bar and clears any leftover slide; nothing drains while downed,
  aiming, crouched, prone or standing still.
- The bar is built once, hides when full, and does no work while hidden.
- Numbers are in `Config.Stamina` and `Config.Movement.Slide`; the rules are pure with tests.

## Check in Studio

   mid-roll; repeat with slide and C. Do you end up crouched or prone?
2. Run the bar empty with Shift held and press C: you go prone. Is that what you want? Keep
   holding Shift: sprint returns at 25 and cuts out again about 2 s later; does that stutter
   feel bad?
3. Slide down Delta's steepest ramp and get downed mid-slide (dev panel "Down me"): can you
   steer, and how long until control returns?
4. A long downhill slide: does the pose hold and the get-up play? Slide into a wall and off
   a ledge.
5. The bar: appears on first use, fades when full, pulses red when winded, does not overlap
   the ability circles.
