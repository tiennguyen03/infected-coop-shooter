# PR #23: Infected spread round a target instead of stacking (not yet played)

- Author: tiennguyen03 (branch `claude/infected-spacing`)
- Head commit reviewed: `f4b8ce97c9022d78f86cdffb89a15fef83d31df3`
- Reviewed: 2026-11-11 (by reading the code; the tests were not run here)

**Verdict: Fix first** (one likely gameplay bug with a small fix; then it needs playing,
because it changes how much damage the horde does).

## What it does

Infected that chase you in a straight line now stop on a ring just inside their attack range
and push away from each other, instead of all standing on the same spot. One that is held
back by the crowd is marked "waiting" so it does not try to climb or sidestep.

## Findings

1. **A Brute cannot hit a player who is lying prone, and may miss a standing one; any
   Infected can miss a player standing a little higher or lower.**
   The ring is measured flat, along the ground: `src/shared/Rules/Spacing.luau:75-76, 82, 90`
   (`ring = attackRange * RingFraction`, 0.8; a body inside the ring "has nowhere to go" and
   stands still). The attack check measures the real distance, height included:
   `src/server/EnemyService.luau:682` (`(targetRoot.Position - zombie.root.Position).Magnitude
   <= zombie.attackRange`). `src/server/EnemySpacing.luau:123-138` passes the attack range
   unchanged and ignores the height difference.
   The Brute is twice normal size (`EnemyDefinitions.luau:90, 94`: Scale 2, AttackRange 5.5),
   so its centre is about 3 studs above a standing player's and about 4.7 above a prone
   player's. It stops 4.4 studs away on the ground, which is about 5.3 to 6.4 studs in real
   distance: on the edge standing, out of reach prone. It then counts as "stuck"
   (`EnemyService.luau:943-954`) and sidesteps 8 studs left and right forever. Before this
   PR it walked right up to the player.
   Trigger: go prone in the open with a Brute chasing you. Also: stand on something 2.5+
   studs high (a car bonnet, a kerb, a ramp) with Walkers around (Walker ring 3.2, range 4).
   How sure: the flat-versus-real mismatch and the sidestep are verified by reading. The
   exact heights depend on Roblox's default body sizes, so: prone case very likely, standing
   case possible. Measure in Studio.
   Smallest fix, in `EnemySpacing.goal` before calling `Spacing.steer`:
   ```lua
   local dy = target.Y - here.Y
   local flatReach = math.sqrt(math.max(attackRange * attackRange - dy * dy, 0))
   ```
   and pass `flatReach` instead of `attackRange`.

2. **Possible: two or more Walkers arriving side by side at thin low cover stop climbing.**
   `src/shared/Rules/Spacing.luau:177`: `waiting = crowded and not travelling and step <
   settings.WaitStep` (2 studs). A body counts as "crowded" when any neighbour is touching
   it, including one beside it (`:131-132`), and its step is already short when it is close
   to the ring (`:112`). "Waiting" turns off the climb (`EnemyService.luau:943-944`).
   Trigger: hug the far side of a jersey barrier or one row of sandbags and let 2+ Walkers
   arrive together: they stand in a row instead of climbing. One Walker alone still climbs.
   How sure: the maths is verified by reading; whether a given piece of cover is thin enough
   is possible. Smallest fix:
   ```lua
   local waiting = crowded and not travelling
       and step < math.min(settings.WaitStep, pull - settings.Deadzone)
   ```

3. **Needs a decision: the horde does less damage.** Only about 6 to 7 Walkers fit on the
   ring; the rest wait behind. That is the point of the PR, but it makes the game easier.
   Compare damage taken with `Config.Enemies.Spacing.Enabled` on and off.

4. Small: with more than 32 Infected in an 18 x 18 stud area, the neighbour list takes the
   first 32 found, not the nearest (`EnemySpacing.luau:108-110`). Some stacking in the
   densest crowds only.

Checked and fine:
- Cost: it runs once per Infected every 0.25 s (not every frame), looks only at nearby
  bodies through a grid, and creates almost no garbage. Fine for the cap of 44.
- The Hocker (ranged) and the Warden (boss) are not steered; lures and pathfinding are
  untouched. With `Enabled = false` the behaviour is exactly as on `main`.
- Nothing is stored per player or per target, so nothing can go stale when a target dies or
  leaves; dead Infected leave the grid within 0.2 s; a run restart clears it.
- No messages to or from players are added. Numbers are in `Config.Enemies.Spacing`, the
  rule is pure with a 261-line test file.
- Small doc mismatch: the `Config` comment on `RingFraction` says "under 1 = can hit", which
  finding 1 shows is not always true.
