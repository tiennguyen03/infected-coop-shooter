# PR #36: Rewards picked from a big panel; the objective reward restocks the squad

- Author: tiennguyen03 (branch `claude/reward-pick`)
- Head commit reviewed: `645b38b130b5b1886fa76d303aa5e13b9a00648b`
- Reviewed: 2026-11-11 (by reading the code; the tests were not run here). Only the part
  added on top of #15 was reviewed; #15's findings are in `PR-15.md`.

**Verdict: Fix first** (no cheat found; a wasted upgrade pick and a key clash need fixing,
and it depends on #40 for its key).

## What it does

You no longer walk into a card to take it: you hold E at a crate and a panel opens with up to
three big cards to choose from. The crate that drops when the hold objective ends now
restocks every living soldier once and offers each player a field upgrade.

## Findings

1. **Two upgrade crates open at once can still waste a pick, or silently replace an
   upgrade.** (This is PR-15 finding 2, still here.) The first upgrade crate stays open 120 s
   and the objective crate lands 10 studs away when the hold ends, so both are open
   together. Each crate's cards are chosen once when it lands
   (`src/server/RequisitionService.luau:425`) and never refreshed, from a list of only 7
   upgrades. `LoadoutService.giveUpgrade` (`src/server/LoadoutService.luau:528-541`) still
   says "ok" for an upgrade you already hold, so `applyCard` (`RequisitionService.luau:492-495`)
   counts the pick as used.
   Trigger: do not open the first crate during the hold; when the second lands, take card X
   from one and X from the other (nothing gained), or take a weapon upgrade from one and a
   different weapon upgrade from the other (the first is replaced with no "REPLACES" warning).
   How sure: verified by reading. Smallest fix in `applyCard`:
   ```lua
   if not player or LoadoutService.hasUpgrade(player, cardId)
       or not LoadoutService.giveUpgrade(player, cardId) then
       return nil
   end
   ```
   Better: after an upgrade is taken, re-roll that player's cards on their other open crates.

2. **Possible: on this branch alone, holding E at a crate also uses a stim.** The crate
   prompt's key is E (`src/shared/Config.luau:809`, with the comment "becomes
   Config.Keys.Interact once claude/keybinds merges"), but E is still the stim key here
   (`src/client/AbilityController.luau:302`). Unless Roblox hides the prompt's key press from
   the game's own key handler, a hurt player who opens a crate also spends a stim.
   How sure: possible (both bindings verified; whether both fire needs a playtest).
   Fix: merge #40 (which moves the stim to V) first, or keep the prompt on another key until
   then.

3. **A player who joins while a crate is open gets no cards and is told "You already took
   yours".** Offers are only made when the crate lands (`RequisitionService.luau:421-429`);
   there is nothing for a player who arrives later, and leaving and rejoining loses the
   offer (`:677-683`). The client shows the wrong message for this case
   (`src/client/ResupplyUI.luau:208-213`). How sure: verified by reading.
   Good news: PR-15 finding 1 is **fixed** for downed and dead players; they now get cards
   and can pick once back up.
   Smallest fix: when a squad player with no offer uses an open crate, create the offer then.

4. Small: after a pause, the panel's "closes in m:ss" is wrong (the server moves the closing
   time, the client does not; `ResupplyUI.luau:261`). Also `onPick` accepts a pick while
   paused. Harmless.

5. Possible, phones only: the panel's full-screen background is a button
   (`src/client/RewardPanel.luau:394-405`) that probably blocks the movement thumbstick and
   closes the panel on any stray touch. Needs a phone or the emulator.

6. Small: the crate's "TAKEN 0/1" counter ignores bots; a downed player is shown "SQUAD
   RESTOCKED" though they were not restocked; `docs/HISTORY.md` says "hold F at a crate" but
   the key is E.

Checked and fine (the pick message, read line by line, `RequisitionService.luau:515-552`):
- Wrong types are dropped. A card you were not offered is refused. You cannot pick twice,
  pick for someone else, pick while downed or dead, from too far away, after the crate
  closed, or with a crate number from an earlier run.
- The squad restock happens once, when the crate lands, not once per player who picks.
- Each player has their own cards and their own panel. Nobody can block the game by not
  choosing: the crate closes by itself. The panel closes if you die, go down, walk away or
  the run restarts, and the mouse is given back each time.
- The game does not pause while you choose (on purpose): you can still move and be hit.
- The rule (`Rules/Reward`) is pure with tests; names go through `Messages` and `Attributes`.
