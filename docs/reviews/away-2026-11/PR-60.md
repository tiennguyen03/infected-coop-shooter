## What it does

It fixes the bugs found in the earlier review notes in this folder: a lobby buy wiping merits,
bots blocking throws or walking off roofs, the Brute missing prone players, the cutscene
skip, objective errors freezing a mission, and more. Each fix is small and in its own file.
Several fixes come with new tests.

## Findings (new problems)

1. **If the cutscene breaks, the helicopter and its rotor sound stay for the whole mission**
   (sure; only on an error, the case the safety net is for). The new `release()` gives back the
   camera, HUD and controls (`src/client/InsertionCutscene.luau:357-381`), but the helicopter
   model, the dust and the looping rotor sound are made inside `play()` (lines 453-459) and
   only `play()`'s own finish removes them. When `play()` errors during setup, line 837 calls
   only `release()`. Trigger: any error in the cutscene after line 455. Fix: remember a small
   clean-up function when the helicopter is made, and call it from `release()`.
2. **Co-op skip: if the last player still watching leaves, the squad waits the full timer**
   (sure; costs time only). The "everyone skipped" check runs only when someone presses skip
   (`src/server/RunManager.luau:130-144`). When a player leaves, line 423 only forgets their
   entry. It also uses a raw `Players.PlayerRemoving` instead of the `Lifecycle` hook that
   RunManager already has (line 361), against the project rules. Fix: in the Lifecycle hook,
   forget the entry and re-run the check.
3. **Bots can still walk off a small roof** (sure; #52-5 only partly fixed). The new floor
   check covers the 6 tries, but when all 6 fail, the fallback spot
   (`src/server/BotService.luau:201`) is used without it. Fix: check the floor there too, else
   stand on the leader's spot.
4. Possible: the fix for "revive slide through a wall" (#39-1) may also switch off the slide in
   normal revives. Any single moving frame sets `nudged = true`
   (`src/shared/ReviveAnimation.luau:216`), including the first frames when a reviver who ran
   up is still slowing down, so they may only turn and never slide to the kneel point.
5. Possible: bots' Anvil may be refused when a squadmate stands on the line
   (`BotService.luau:990`). The Anvil is aimed with binoculars, not thrown, so a body on the
   throw line should not matter.
6. Small: `crate.shown[player]` (`src/server/RequisitionService.luau:328`) is not cleared
   when a player leaves (lines 723-729 clear the rest). It only lasts until the crate closes.
7. Possible: `ObjectiveService` still calls each objective's `position()` and `progress()`
   without protection (lines 135, 145). An error there would still stall the mission, the same
   kind of problem #37-3 was about.
8. Lobby text, small: if a late merit payment lands during a buy, the "bought" line does not
   show (`ArmsRackUI.luau:861` compares merits with before). The new refusal reasons "busy" and
   "unreadable" show the generic refusal text.
9. Docs: `docs/PROGRESSION.md:608` still says 70 Infected placed at start (now 30, a few per
   frame). PROGRESSION does not say that skipping now shortens the insertion.
