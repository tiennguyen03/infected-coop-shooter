## What it does

The soldier now wears a plain helmet with separate night-vision goggles. Pressing N (night
vision, added in #48) swings the goggles down over the eyes, and other players see it.

## Findings

1. **Do not press Merge on this by itself.** It is built on #49, which is built on #48,
   which is built on the integration branch (#44). Against `main` it is 91 commits. Nova's
   own goggles work is the last two. Merge order: the integration branch's contents, then
   #48, #49, then this.

2. **Small: the new message has no rate limit and does not check that the sender is
   alive.** `src/server/NightVisionService.luau:15-23`. The server checks the type (true or
   false) and only ever sets a flag on the sender's own soldier, so nothing can be gained.
   A cheating client could flip it in a tight loop, which sends an update to the other
   players each time; a dead player can lower the goggles on their body. Cosmetic only.
   How sure: verified by reading. Optional fix: ignore more than about 10 changes a second
   per player (but always let "off" through).

3. **Possible: a squadmate's goggles can look raised while their night vision is on.**
   `src/client/NightVision.luau:179-183` records the goggles as "in place" before checking
   that the pose worked; `Goggles.pose` (`src/shared/Goggles.luau:44-48`) quietly does
   nothing if the goggles' parts have not arrived yet, and it is never retried.
   Trigger: join late, or walk far from a squadmate and back, while they have night vision
   on. How sure: possible (parts normally arrive together). Smallest fix: make `pose` say
   whether it worked, and keep trying until it does.

4. **Small: the goggles are recognised by the mesh id of a Roblox catalog item the team
   does not own** (`src/shared/Config.luau:117`; `Goggles.luau:82`). If its maker re-uploads
   or removes it, the goggles are no longer found: they stay down over every soldier's eyes
   and N shows nothing, with no warning in the Output. The soldier's look now depends on two
   third-party catalog items instead of one. Smallest fix: print one warning when a soldier
   has accessories but no goggles were found.

Checked and fine:
- The server keeps no state for this; the flag lives on the soldier and resets on respawn.
- The names are in `Remotes` and `Attributes`; the numbers are in `Config.NightVision.Goggles`.
- Every lookup of the goggles' parts is guarded, so a missing accessory causes no error.
- Cost: one loop over at most four soldiers' goggles, moving them only during the 0.35 s flip.
- In first person your own goggles are hidden with the rest of your body.
- Bots and lobby figures keep their goggles stowed. No change to `docs/OWNER.md` here.
- Nova's note says this one was played ("works").
