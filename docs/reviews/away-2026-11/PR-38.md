## What it does

Roll moves from Shift + Left Ctrl to pressing Shift twice quickly. Slide becomes "hold C or
Left Ctrl while sprinting" and lasts as long as the key is held. C and Left Ctrl both crouch,
and prone moves to Z.

## Findings

1. **Decision: please confirm the owner chose these keys.** The PR rewrites the rule in
   `docs/OWNER.md` from "not decided yet. Do not change the bindings without them" to "roll
   is Shift twice; slide is holding C or Ctrl while sprinting (owner, 2026-11-11)". This
   review cannot tell whether Tien said that on 2026-11-11 or an agent assumed it. If Tien
   did, fine; if not, this PR and its change to `docs/OWNER.md` should wait.

2. **A roll no longer needs a sprint, so it can happen by accident and while aiming.**
   On #21 the roll was only tried while sprinting. Here any two Shift presses within 0.3 s
   start one (`src/client/MovementController.luau`, the `LeftShift` branch in `init`;
   `Config.Movement.Roll.DoubleTap = 0.3`), as long as you are moving at all
   (`tryDash` only refuses when standing still, downed, knocked or out of stamina).
   Trigger: tap Shift twice to "re-start" a sprint, or while walking and aiming down sights:
   you roll and lose 20 stamina. How sure: verified by reading; how often it happens by
   accident needs playing. Smallest fix if unwanted: also require `not aiming` (and perhaps
   a minimum speed) before the roll.

3. Small: during a slide, if you let go of Shift and press the other low key (C while
   holding Ctrl, or the reverse), the stance code sees "not sprinting" and toggles crouch
   in the middle of the slide (`src/client/StanceController.luau:124-134`). Possible; needs
   a playtest.

4. Small, good news for #21's findings: because a slide now ends when the key is released,
   a player who is downed mid-slide will usually stop sliding when they let go (PR-21
   finding 1 still needs its fix), and an empty stamina bar now makes C crouch instead of
   go prone (PR-21 finding 2 becomes milder).

Checked and fine:
- A tap still slides for at least 0.35 s (`Slide.MinHold`); the slide still ends by itself
  when it runs out of speed or after 6 s.
- The roll still reports to the server for its short melee protection exactly as before;
  nothing new is sent.
- The new values (`DoubleTap`, `MinHold`) are in `Config.Movement`.

## Check in Studio

1. Sprint, let go of Shift and press it again quickly: do you roll when you did not mean to?
2. Walk while aiming down sights and tap Shift twice: do you roll out of your aim?
3. Sprint and hold C: you slide until you let go. Tap C: a short slide. Same with Left Ctrl.
4. Standing still: C or Ctrl crouches, Z goes prone, Shift stands you up.
5. Start a slide, let go of Shift, press the other low key (finding 3).
