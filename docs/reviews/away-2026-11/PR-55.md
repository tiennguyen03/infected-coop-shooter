## What it does

On the lobby ship's flight deck you used to board a helicopter by standing in a small yellow
square beside its door. Now the whole yellow circle painted round each helicopter is the
boarding zone, and the "HELO · SPOT n" sign stands on the circle's edge.

## Findings

No bugs found.

1. The painted ring and the server's check read the same number
   (`Config.Lobby.Launch.BoardRadius = 14`, `src/shared/Config.luau:2839`), so what you see is
   what counts. The ring is the same size as before (28 across), so nothing on the deck moved.
2. The check is a small tested rule (`src/shared/Rules/Manifest.luau:98-106`, 3 new tests in
   `tests/Manifest.spec.luau`); it is done on the server in `LaunchService.present`, so a
   player cannot fake being aboard. All players are checked, not just one.
3. Rings do not touch each other (spots are 45 studs apart, rings 28 across), stay on the deck
   (deck edge at X ±35, ring X -26 to 2), and end 21 studs before the ramp's top, so a player
   coming up the ramp does not land inside a ring.
4. Nothing still uses the old `BoardHalfSize` or `spot.board` (searched the whole branch).
5. Possible, small (a feel question, not a bug): the rings sit on the line down the deck. A
   player walking aft to spot 1 straight down the middle (X about -12) passes through the
   rings of spots 4, 3 and 2 and is briefly "aboard" each one, which may start that bird's
   20-second countdown on their screen. Walking along the island side (X above 2) avoids it.

## Check in Studio

1. Stand anywhere inside a ring, including behind the helicopter: the boarding panel appears.
2. Step just outside the yellow paint: the panel goes away.
3. Walk from the ramp to spot 1 down the middle of the deck: see whether passing spots 4, 3, 2
   flashes their countdown, and whether that is annoying.
4. Two players in one ring: both are listed, and a full squad shortens the countdown.
