## What it does

Calling in the ATV used to show a helicopter hovering and lowering it on a rope. Now the
helicopter flies straight over without stopping, drops a wooden crate the size of the vehicle,
and the crate falls open around the ATV (Tien's idea). It is only a look on each player's
screen; the ATV itself still appears at the same moment on the server.

## Findings

No bugs found in the crate drop.

1. Fine: all crate pieces are made by the existing helper, which anchors them and turns off
   collision and touch (`src/client/CallInArrivalFX.luau:127-140`), so nobody can trip on a
   falling board. They live inside the arrival's own model, which is removed when the arrival
   ends, and the one listener it adds is disconnected (`CallInArrivalFX.luau` crate style,

   when the ATV appears 7.5 seconds later. With the crate it looks nicer, but the ATV can still
   appear inside a teammate or a parked ATV.

   down. ATV's yours."); the commit says they are pending approval, and the lore commit says
   Nova approved them. Fine either way, just confirm.
