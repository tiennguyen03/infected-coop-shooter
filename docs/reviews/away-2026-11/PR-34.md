## What it does

The Doorman sentry is now a modelled machine gun on a tripod instead of five grey blocks. It
turns in two pieces (the cradle turns left and right, the gun tips up and down) and its
tracers leave the modelled muzzle. A `Config` switch (`CallIns.Doorman.Model = 1`) brings
the old blocks back, and they are also used if the new meshes have not loaded.

## Findings

No bugs found in the code.

1. **Small behaviour change: the sentry now "sees" from lower down.** It looks for targets
   and fires from 2.6 studs above the ground instead of 3.2
   (`src/server/SentryService.luau:288-289`, `Config.CallIns.Doorman.PitchHeight`). Low
   cover in front of it will block more shots than before. The PR's note says so. Worth a
   test with the sentry placed behind a jersey barrier.

2. Small, looks only: the gun only tips 40 degrees up or down
   (`SentryService.luau:167-168`, `PitchLimit = 40`), but the shot still goes to the target.
   Against a target steeply above or below, the tracer will leave the muzzle at an angle to
   the barrel.

3. Small, project rule: the Part body's numbers and the mesh names are constants in
   `SentryService.luau:25-27`, while the model's numbers are on the Doorman's `Config` row.
   Fine as it is.

Checked and fine:
- The aiming maths (turn, tip, where the cradle and gun sit) is consistent with the starting
  position, and a target directly above the gun is handled (it just keeps its last aim).
- Like the old body, the new one is fixed in place and has no collision, so nobody gets
  stuck on it. All three pieces are removed together when the sentry expires or a new run
  starts.
- If any of the three meshes is missing, the pieces already made are cleaned up and the old
  body is used. No error.
- Damage, range, fire rate, one sentry per player and who can deploy are unchanged.
- Size: 2,300 triangles in three meshes, under the 5,000 limit it is counted against, at most
  four in a match. No lights or particles added.
- The art comes from the generator (`assets/3d/build/doorman.py`).
