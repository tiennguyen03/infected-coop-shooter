# Bots walk and use their kit (Tien's agent, 2026-11-11)

**Changed:** bots no longer teleport to catch up. They walk to their slot (straight when it is
close and in sight, else a PathfindingService path to the leader), sprint at 30 beyond 45
studs, and teleport only when over 30 studs from the leader and stalled for 6 s (3 s after
three paths not found) or 80 studs below him; they land on the first slot no living player
faces. They stim at 40% health, throw frags at 4+ Infected, and each carries one fire mission
(Hammer / Anvil / Hammer) for 8+ Infected or a Brute / boss, with a radio subtitle. Rules:
`src/shared/Rules/BotBrain.luau`; numbers: `Config.Bots`; body: `BotService`.
**Verified:** stylua, selene, both rojo builds, luau-lsp, Lune (153 tests, 17 new in
`tests/BotBrain.spec.luau`). Not playtested: Studio was in use.
**Heads-up:** `KitService.throwFrag` and `KitService.dose` are the shared throw and dose (the
player paths call them after their checks); `KitService.blast` and
`AirStrikeService.fireMission` take `Player?` (nil = a bot, no kill credit);
`CallInService.fireMissionFor(member, id, point)` is the bot's throw; RequisitionService
requires BotService (`BotService.restock` on a Restock card). `Config.Bots.TeleportBeyond` is
gone. The radio line `Config.Bots.CallIn.Radio` is a placeholder for the lore developer.
**Next:** a playtest: bots through doors and up stairs, how often the server prints
"was stuck and rejoined the squad", whether three bots' fire missions carry too hard, whether
the Anvil's 5 s delay lands on a moving Brute. Then a throw clip for the bot and vaulting.
